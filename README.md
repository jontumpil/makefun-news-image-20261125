A one-page newspaper for Wednesday 25 November 2026: 49 stories from
7 regions, every story dated 25 November 2026 and written from its
named source, with a DATE CHECK box, and four illustrated lead cards.
Rendered with the Python standard library only.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

This renders `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) from `stories.py`
through the pure-stdlib renderer in `draw_frontpage.py`. No third
party package is needed at run time.

## What is inside

- Title: Wednesday 25 November 2026: 49 stories, 7 regions, Hormuz
  tankers mined, Shenzhou-24 flies first foreign astronaut, US
  tariffs hit 14 partners
- 49 stories, 7 per region: North America, Latin America, Europe,
  Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific, Science & Culture.
- Every story carries a 40-70 word summary written from its source
  article, plus its source and a C (CONFIRMED) or R (REPORTED) tag.
- Four illustrated lead cards: Shenzhou-24 (`assets/shenzhou.png`),
  Hormuz tankers (`assets/hormuz.png`), US tariffs
  (`assets/tariffs.png`), Solomon Islands treaty
  (`assets/solomon.png`).
- The DATE CHECK box states that every story is dated Wednesday
  25 November 2026.
